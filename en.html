FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

WHAT TO KNOW BEFORE MAKING AN ENQUIRY

Key information about assignments and working with us at a glance.

Where does S&Motion operate?

We undertake assignments across Europe. Location, scope and timing are agreed individually.

Which equipment and systems does S&Motion service?

We service conveyor belts and conveyor systems, production equipment, electrical control systems, sensors and actuators.

Are short-notice or urgent assignments possible?

Yes, short-notice and urgent assignments are possible subject to availability. Please contact us by phone or email.

Which types of assignment are available?

We provide individual repair and service assignments, regular maintenance and longer-term equipment support.

Which details are helpful for an initial enquiry?

Please provide the type of equipment or machine, a description of the fault or task, available photos and technical documents, and your preferred location and date.

How is completed work documented?

After completion, you receive a work or service report with photographic documentation.
